Exploration and Discovery is a key component of what I love about terraria. Sometimes, however, I find myself feeling bored of the current configuration of biomes, the same set of picked through caves, or the same stretch of familiar dungeon that I have already spent countless hours in. I find this to be especially true when playing with friends, at certain stages of the game, some players find specific rare or limited items and the rest are left to wonder if another even exists in the world. Certain items and structures arent even guaranteed to spawn at all. While you can always start a new world a bring your character

Dynamic Worlds is my project to provide  
Terraria Progress has three components:
1. Character
-follows your terraria character, separate from world includes:
--Gear (Tools, Weapons, Armor, Accessories)
--Permanent Buffs (Health, Mana Stars, Shimmer Buffs, etc)
These are largely not touched by dynamic worlds as they are attached to your character file, not world file so they will stick with your character between worlds
2. World Flags
- A "Checklist" of events/accomplishments tied to the world which trigger progression
-- Boss downs
-- Event Completions
-- Breaking certain tiles (Evil Orbs, Demon Altars)
-- World Buffs (Advanced Combat Techniques)
Dynamic Worlds restores these flags on world regeneration and retriggers their associated events.
3. Builds
- Placed tiles give the player progress in the form of a massive variety of ever increasing utility that is lost upon entering a new world. These utilities include:
--increasing crafting capabilties
--access to town NPCs
--storage space for items
--protection from dangerous biomes or events
--Arenas for fighting bosses
--sources of powerful temporary buffs
--renewable resource farming
--faster travel between biomes (Hellevators, Pylons)
--attachment to the world from cool builds
Dynamic Worlds offers four distinct tools to allow players to preserve as much of this progress as they would like through world regenerations.
