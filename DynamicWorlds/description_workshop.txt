[b][h1]Dynamic Worlds[/h1][/b]
[i]Fresh terrain, same playthrough.[/i]

Dynamic Worlds is a single-player tModLoader utility mod that regenerates your world's terrain while preserving the parts of the playthrough you actually care about: progression, protected builds, important tiles, NPC housing, and preserved storage.

Run [code]/regenworld [seed][/code] to save the current world, enter a loading screen, generate fresh terrain, restore preserved data, and automatically load back into the regenerated world.

---

[h2]At A Glance[/h2]
[list]
[*] Manual world regeneration with [code]/regenworld [seed][/code]
[*] Real loading-screen regen flow instead of freezing live gameplay
[*] Configurable scheduled regen that can be enabled, disabled, and timed in in-game days
[*] Three world tools for preserving tiles, clearing space, and saving full structures
[*] Preserved NPC housing, bed spawn handling, and vanilla pylon repair
[/list]

[h2]What Gets Preserved[/h2]
[list]
[*] Hardmode, game mode, evil type if configured, ore tiers, boss flags, event flags, invasions, and world identity
[*] Town NPC roster, names, respawn positions, and preserved housing when a valid home survives
[*] Anchored tiles, structure zones, and chest or dresser contents inside preserved areas
[*] Bed spawn handling, with safe fallback to world spawn if the bed did not survive
[/list]

[h2]World Tools[/h2]

[b]Reality Anchor[/b]
[list]
[*] Preserves exact tiles, walls, liquids, wires, actuators, and container contents
[*] Left-click a tile to anchor or unanchor it
[*] Click and drag to apply anchoring to a whole rectangle
[*] Best for beds, storage rooms, wiring hubs, farms, and hand-built terrain
[/list]

[b]Reality Eraser[/b]
[list]
[*] Marks tiles that should be cleared to empty space after every regen
[*] Left-click a tile to mark or unmark it
[*] Click and drag to apply erasure to a whole rectangle
[*] Great for tunnels, arenas, access shafts, and keeping surface approaches clear
[/list]

[b]Structure Anchor[/b]
[list]
[*] Preserves an entire build as one zone instead of tile-by-tile
[*] Click and drag to create a structure zone
[*] Shift-click inside a zone to remove it
[*] Restores the structure onto the new terrain and bridges small support gaps underneath
[*] Floating island builds stay floating instead of getting a giant dirt pillar to the ground
[/list]

Hold any world tool to see anchors, erasures, and structure zones together.

[h2]Commands[/h2]
[list]
[*] [code]/regenworld [seed][/code] - run a regeneration manually
[*] [code]/snap[/code] - print the current progression snapshot and scheduler status
[*] [code]/dwtools[/code] - receive the three world tools again
[*] [code]/dwinfo[/code] - inspect saved anchors, erasures, and structure zones
[*] [code]/dwzone [list|clear|clearall] [id][/code] - manage structure zones
[*] [code]/clearzones[/code] - remove all structure zones
[*] [code]/hardmode [on|off][/code] and [code]/down <bossOrEvent>[/code] - optional admin or testing tools
[/list]

[h2]Config[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Enable Scheduled Regen[/b] - turn automatic regen on or off
[*] [b]Scheduled Regen Interval (Days)[/b] - choose how many in-game days pass between scheduled regens
[*] [b]Preserve Evil Type[/b] - keep the old world evil after regen, or turn it off to allow a reroll
[*] [b]Randomize World Each Regen[/b] - use a fresh seed every time or keep regens repeatable
[*] [b]Allow Cheats[/b] - enable admin and testing actions such as progression commands and manual tool actions
[/list]

[h2]Compatibility And Limitations[/h2]
[list]
[*] Single-player only
[*] Works best when the same mod list stays installed before and after regen
[*] Preserved chest and dresser contents are intended to support modded items through tModLoader serialization
[*] Modded tiles, walls, and town NPCs can often survive regen if their source mods are still present
[*] Compatibility is weaker for custom pylons, unusual tile entities, and major worldgen-overhaul mods
[/list]

[h2]FAQ[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Does regen happen live while I keep playing?[/b] No. The mod saves and exits to a loading screen, regenerates the world there, then loads you back in automatically.
[*] [b]Can it change my world evil?[/b] Yes. Turn off [b]Preserve Evil Type[/b], then regen to let the new world choose its evil.
[*] [b]What about pylons?[/b] Preserved vanilla pylons are repaired after regen so they function again, but they are not currently auto-moved to a matching biome.
[*] [b]Will it work on older worlds?[/b] If the world still loads in your current Terraria/tModLoader setup, Dynamic Worlds can still snapshot and regenerate it. It uses current worldgen, not historical 1.3 generation.
[*] [b]Will Structure Anchor generate a whole hill around my house?[/b] Not fully. It moves the structure onto new terrain and bridges small gaps; preserve the surrounding terrain too if you want the build embedded.
[/list]
