[b][h1]Dynamic Worlds – Persistent World Progression + World Regeneration[/h1][/b]

Dynamic Worlds is a lightweight utility mod that lets you regenerate your Terraria world layout WITHOUT losing your bosses, Hardmode status, ore tiers, or event progression.

Whether you want a fresh world layout or a new biome distribution — but don't want to repeat early-game progression — this mod preserves your world's identity and upgrades while refreshing terrain.

---

[h2]⭐ Features[/h2]

[b]• World Regeneration with Progress Kept[/b]
Use [code]/regenworld[/code] to regenerate the world terrain while restoring all progression afterward. Bosses, Hardmode, ores, invasions, and town NPCs are all preserved. Pass an optional seed for a repeatable result:
[code]/regenworld[/code]
[code]/regenworld 12345[/code]
[code]/regenworld myseedname[/code]
After teleporting, you receive [b]10 seconds of Featherfall[/b] so you land safely.

[b]• Automatic Regeneration Scheduler[/b]
The world automatically regenerates every [b]7 in-game days[/b]. A spooky countdown broadcasts in chat as the day approaches:
[list]
[*] [i]3 days out:[/i] "☠ The ground trembles. The world will regenerate in 3 days... ☠"
[*] [i]1 day out:[/i] "☠ Something wicked approaches. The world regenerates TOMORROW. ☠"
[*] [i]Dawn of regen day:[/i] "☠ The world stirs... regeneration begins at midnight. ☠"
[*] [i]Midnight:[/i] The world tears itself apart and is reborn.
[/list]
The day counter saves with your world — it survives restarts. Check days remaining with [b]/snap[/b].

[b]• Reality Anchor – Persistent Structures[/b]
Mark tiles you want to [b]keep[/b] across every regeneration. The Reality Anchor is given to you automatically when you first enter a world.
[list]
[*] [b]Left-click[/b] any tile while holding the Reality Anchor to anchor or unanchor it. Anchored tiles glow while the item is held.
[*] [b]Click and drag[/b] to anchor or unanchor a whole rectangular area at once.
[*] [b]Chests and dressers[/b] save their exact contents — items included.
[*] [b]Anchor your bed[/b] to preserve your personal spawn point. The mod validates the bed tile survived before reasserting your spawn.
[*] [b]Right-click[/b] the Reality Anchor in your inventory to restore all anchored tiles immediately, without running a full regen. The item is [b]never consumed[/b].
[*] The anchor limit scales with boss progression: [b]5,000 tiles[/b] at the start, up to [b]100,000[/b] after Moon Lord.
[*] The tooltip shows your current count, cap, fill level, and whether your bed is anchored.
[/list]

[b]• Reality Eraser – Guaranteed Empty Tiles[/b]
Mark tiles to be [b]cleared to empty space[/b] after every world regen. The Reality Eraser is given to you alongside the Reality Anchor.
[list]
[*] [b]Left-click[/b] any tile while holding the Reality Eraser to mark or unmark it for erasure.
[*] [b]Click and drag[/b] to mark or unmark a whole rectangular area at once.
[*] Erasure runs [b]before[/b] anchored tiles are restored — anchors always win over erased positions.
[*] [b]Right-click[/b] the Reality Eraser in your inventory to immediately clear all marked tiles now. The item is [b]never consumed[/b].
[*] The erasure limit is much higher than the anchor limit: [b]50,000 tiles[/b] at the start, up to [b]1,000,000[/b] after Moon Lord.
[/list]

[b]• Guide NPC Dialogue[/b]
Ask the Guide NPC about the Reality Anchor. Click the [b]Crafting[/b] button during Guide chat to open the Reality Anchor info page — he'll explain how anchoring, chest persistence, and manual restore work.

[b]• Player Position Persistence[/b]
Your last position in the world is saved when you exit and restored when you return — you always pick up exactly where you left off.

[b]• World Progress Persistence[/b]
Automatically saves bosses, invasions, Hardmode, ore tiers, progression flags, and town NPC roster to a JSON file linked to your world.

[b]• Hardmode Toggle[/b]
[code]/hardmode on[/code]
[code]/hardmode off[/code]

[b]• Boss/Event Flags Editing[/b]
[code]/down eye[/code]
[code]/down plantera[/code]

[b]• Snapshot Debugging[/b]
View world progression and days until next auto-regen:
[code]/snap[/code]
