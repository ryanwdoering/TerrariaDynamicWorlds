Dynamic Worlds – Persistent World Progression + World Regeneration

Dynamic Worlds is a lightweight utility mod that lets you regenerate your Terraria world layout WITHOUT losing your bosses, Hardmode status, ore tiers, or event progression.

Whether you want a fresh world layout or a new biome distribution — but don't want to repeat early-game progression — this mod preserves your world's identity and upgrades while refreshing terrain.

---

⭐ Features

• World Regeneration with Progress Kept
Use /regenworld to regenerate the world terrain while restoring all progression afterward. Bosses, Hardmode, ores, invasions, and town NPCs are all preserved. Pass an optional seed to get a repeatable result:
  /regenworld
  /regenworld 12345
  /regenworld myseedname
After teleporting, you receive 10 seconds of Featherfall so you land safely.

• Automatic Regeneration Scheduler
The world automatically regenerates every 7 in-game days. A spooky countdown broadcasts in chat as the day approaches:
  - 3 days out: "☠ The ground trembles. The world will regenerate in 3 days... ☠"
  - 1 day out: "☠ Something wicked approaches. The world regenerates TOMORROW. ☠"
  - Dawn of regen day: "☠ The world stirs... regeneration begins at midnight. ☠"
  - Midnight: The world tears itself apart and is reborn.
The day counter saves with your world — it survives restarts. Check days remaining with /snap.

• Reality Anchor – Persistent Structures
Mark tiles you want to keep across every regeneration. The Reality Anchor is given to you automatically when you first enter a world.
  - Left-click any tile while holding the Reality Anchor to anchor or unanchor it. Anchored tiles glow while the item is held.
  - Click and drag to anchor or unanchor a whole rectangular area at once.
  - Chests and dressers save their exact contents — items included.
  - Anchor your bed to preserve your personal spawn point. The mod validates the bed tile survived before reasserting your spawn.
  - Right-click the Reality Anchor in your inventory to restore all anchored tiles immediately, without running a full regen. The item is never consumed.
  - The anchor limit scales with boss progression: 5,000 tiles at the start, up to 100,000 after Moon Lord.
  - The tooltip shows your current count, cap, fill level, and whether your bed is anchored.

• Reality Eraser – Guaranteed Empty Tiles
Mark tiles to be cleared to empty space after every world regen. The Reality Eraser is given to you alongside the Reality Anchor.
  - Left-click any tile while holding the Reality Eraser to mark or unmark it for erasure.
  - Click and drag to mark or unmark a whole rectangular area at once.
  - Erasure runs before anchored tiles are restored — anchors always win over erased positions.
  - Right-click the Reality Eraser in your inventory to immediately clear all marked tiles now. The item is never consumed.
  - The erasure limit is much higher than the anchor limit: 50,000 tiles at the start, up to 1,000,000 after Moon Lord.

• Guide NPC Dialogue
Ask the Guide NPC about the Reality Anchor. Click the Crafting button during Guide chat to open the Reality Anchor info page — he'll explain how anchoring, chest persistence, and manual restore work.

• Player Position Persistence
Your last position in the world is saved when you exit and restored when you return — you always pick up exactly where you left off.

• World Progress Persistence
Automatically saves bosses, invasions, Hardmode, ore tiers, progression flags, and town NPC roster to a JSON file linked to your world.

• Hardmode Toggle
  /hardmode on
  /hardmode off

• Boss/Event Flags Editing
  /down eye
  /down plantera

• Snapshot Debugging
View world progression and days until next auto-regen:
  /snap
